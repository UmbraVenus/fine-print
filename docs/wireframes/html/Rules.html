Claims

5What the words promise

Rate claims

A rate like “as low as 7.99% APR” needs the full APR range and any conditions, like autopay.

Applies to personal loans and credit cards · Flagged as Critical

Don’tLoans as low as 7.99% APR
DoFixed rates 7.99%–29.99% APR with autopay

Credit score claims

“No impact to your credit score” is only true for checking your rate. Applying is a hard credit check.

Applies to all products · Flagged as Caution

Don’tNo impact to your credit score!
DoChecking your rate won't affect your credit score. Applying may.

Trigger terms

Some phrases, like “no annual fee”, require the APR and other key terms, even though they sound like good news.

Applies to credit cards · Flagged as Critical

Don’tNo annual fee. Apply now.
DoNo annual fee. 21.24%–29.24% variable APR.

Savings claims

A savings claim needs real evidence, and its exclusions can't be hidden in fine print.

Applies to all products · Flagged as Critical

Don’tMembers save $4,200 on average
Do2025 borrowers who consolidated saved $4,200 on average. Results vary.

Guarantees

No guaranteed approval, and no rates most people can't actually get.

Applies to all products · Flagged as Critical

Don’tGUARANTEED approval!!!
DoCheck your rate in minutes

Required elements

3Things that must be on the ad

Mortgage identifiers

Mortgage ads show the NMLS ID and the Equal Housing logo.

Applies to mortgage · Flagged as Critical

Don’tGet prequalified for your dream home
DoGet prequalified for your dream homeNMLS ID 123456 · Equal Housing Lender

Affiliate disclosure

Affiliate content says clearly that the publisher is paid by ClearPath.

Applies to affiliate content · Flagged as Critical

Don’tThe best loans of 2026
DoThe best loans of 2026We earn a fee from partners like ClearPath.

Paid testimonials

A paid testimonial or influencer post is labeled as a paid partnership.

Applies to affiliate content · Flagged as Critical

Don’tI paid off my cards in a month with ClearPath
DoPaid partnership with ClearPathI paid off my cards in a month with ClearPath

Presentation

1How the ad looks

Clear and conspicuous

A disclosure is a readable size, close to the claim it explains, and visible without scrolling. It can't fix a claim that's false on its own.

Applies to all products · Flagged as Caution

Don’t7.99% APRWith autopay. Terms apply.
Do7.99%–29.99% APRWith autopay. Autopay is not required.
