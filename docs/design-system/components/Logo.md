# Logo

The Fine Print wordmark: one bold word pair with the P under a mint lens; `variant="mark"` is the lens and P alone.

- Header: wordmark at `size={22}`, left of the workspace switcher (ClearPath Financial).
- Favicon and tight spaces: `variant="mark"`, or the `fineprint-mark.svg` asset.
- Outside the app (slides, README, social cards): use the outlined `fineprint-wordmark.svg` from Logos, not a screenshot.
- The lens is `mint` with an `ink` outline; the P is always `ink`, never white, so it reads on the mint.
- It renders as `role="img"` named "Fine Print". Don't add a second visible label beside it.
- Don't recolor the lens yellow: yellow means "flagged" in this product.
