### What still differs from the wireframes

- **Good and checks-run counts** are computed from the rules (see BLOCKED.md), so 03, 05 and 07 show one fewer Good and one fewer check than the wireframes. Example: Cash back card shows "4 Good · 6 checks run", where the wireframe has 5 and 7.
- **05-Review** shows a review in progress (#1 Kept, one finding from you). The seed starts before any of that, and `screenshots/final/05-review.png` recreates that state by clicking.
- **06-What changed**: "Resubmitted today" instead of "40 min ago" (dates by day). The resolved headlines are computed from the change ("Added “ClearPath Home Loans · NMLS ID 123456”.") instead of the wireframe's handwritten "NMLS ID added under the banner.".
- **07-Other formats** is a reference sheet, not a route: emails, landing pages and blog posts render inside Review and Pre-check. Consolidation page's fee finding is pinned once, on the claim, not also on the footer qualifier, because a finding has one layer. Emails have a thin brand-colored bar at the top, so their thumbnails can be told apart.
- **01-My ads**: the wireframe shows a "v1" chip on Holiday debt email. CLAUDE.md asks for a chip only when the version is above 1, so there's none.
- **04-Queue**: Fixed rate Instagram is due "Oct 26" (3 business days), where the wireframe shows Oct 24, a Saturday.
- **08-History**: every number is computed, so the totals and trends differ from the wireframe's (about 2,000 approved ads instead of 1,284). There are no SMS ads because there is no SMS template.
- **10-Add a rule** has a fourth "Affiliate content" checkbox (see BLOCKED.md).
- **The decision bar** sticks to the bottom of the window, so Approve is always in reach. At the end of the page it sits exactly where the wireframe puts it.
- **Rows per page** is an antd Select, not a native select. The format filter also lists "Instagram post", which only affiliates use.
- [x] **M8 · README**: what it is, with the headline number; how to run; how to deploy (Vercel + Turso + env vars); assumptions; what's next; and a short tour script. Seeding now uses batched multi-row inserts (about 190 statements in total), so Reset is quick on Turso too. `vercel.json` bundles the `server/` package with the API function.

## After the milestones

- Fixed: the local Flask server didn't read `.env` (added `python-dotenv`).
- Fixed: "Go to" page took typed numbers past the last page as their last valid prefix ("300" went to page 30). It now checks on Enter and says "Pick a page from 1 to N".
- Fixed: a page that failed to load left a blank screen. Dependencies are pre-bundled at dev-server startup, and an error screen retries once, then offers Reload.
- Pre-check: "Use this wording", "Edit on ad" and a manual "Mark resolved", which the reviewer sees. Download ad (PNG) for approved ads. See BLOCKED.md.
  - Checks: `e2e/loop.spec.ts` (use the wording, edit on ad, mark resolved and reopen, the reviewer sees it, PNG download at full size) and new server tests (resolve and reopen, drop-in wording, the resolved mark kept through a live Re-check).
- Headlines shrink to fit around the art (down to 60%), measured on the real layout, so longer wording never runs into a shape. If shrinking isn't enough, the shape is hidden: words win over decoration. History filler art moved to the bottom-right corner; the circle over Fixed rate Instagram's copy is gone. Checked on 161 social ads: none needed to shrink or hide.
- Suggested fixes are only the words to use, with a copy button in the box. "Swap" (renamed from "Use this wording") sits at the right of the box, after Copy and in line with Mark resolved, when the words are a drop-in replacement; "Edit on ad" and "Add a note" are quiet text buttons on every card, so each card has one clear button in the box and one in the row. The live check returns words only and forgives near-miss quotes (curly quotes, dashes, case).
- "approved on first review" trend tooltip reads "vs. 45% last month", so "12 pts" (percentage points) explains itself.
- Fixed: two first requests arriving together could both run a new migration ("duplicate column name"), and a half-finished one failed on every restart. Setup now runs one request at a time, and a column that's already there is skipped.
- The error page has "Back to Queue" (or My ads), and shows the error itself when running locally.
- Pre-check has Previous / "1 of 2" / Next, like Review: it steps through the marketer's ads that need attention (Draft and Changes required), in My ads order.
- Reviewer findings that put words from the ad in quotation marks are pinned to them (a person pin, so it's never confused with a numbered auto finding): on Review, on the marketer's Pre-check (with Edit on ad), and in What changed, where they count as resolved once the words are gone. Nothing quoted, or words not on the ad: plain text, as before. Check: `e2e/reviewer-quotes.spec.ts` and `test_quoted_words_in_a_reviewer_finding_pin_it_to_the_ad`.

- Stat cards are clickable: on My ads and the Queue they filter the table to their rows (click again for all; the active card has a teal border); "you reviewed this week" opens History.
